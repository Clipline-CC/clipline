> tracking and remain unticked by repository convention.

channel picker on top of Nightly 0.1.56), but stop presenting `v0.1.57` as Latest.

Stable keep one version sequence, so Nightly also becomes 1.0.0.

approved merge `283d381` with a 1.0.0 version identity.
