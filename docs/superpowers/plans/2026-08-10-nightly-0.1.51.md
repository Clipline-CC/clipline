**Release-worthy user-visible changes since 0.1.50:**

- Settings → Storage adds an opt-in **Auto-delete oldest clips** toggle. Quota stays non-destructive
  by default; when enabled, Clipline frees room by deleting oldest managed clips before locking
  recording, while protecting active recordings and in-progress uploads.
- Background quota cleanup refreshes the Library, ignores linked child directories under the media
  root, and serializes overlapping collectors so concurrent recorder generations cannot over-delete.
- Appearance gains a pink theme, with accent fills kept legible against the pink surfaces.
